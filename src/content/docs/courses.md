## Modules and lessons

A course is a sequence of modules, each holding several short lessons. Modules
are ordered so that every lesson only uses what earlier lessons taught.

## Lesson steps

A lesson mixes teaching steps with graded exercises. Teaching steps introduce
a word, a character, or a grammar point. Graded steps ask you to produce or
recognize it. Only graded steps affect your review schedule — reading an
explanation never changes when a card comes back.

## Testing out

If a module covers material you already know, use "test out". Answer the
questions correctly and the module unlocks without working through every
lesson.
