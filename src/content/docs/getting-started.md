## Try it without an account

You can take a full lesson before signing up. Pick a language, work through
the lesson, and see whether the pace suits you. Nothing is saved until you
create an account.

## Create an account

Signing up saves your progress and your review schedule across devices. You
can sign in with an email address or an existing Google account.

## Pick your starting point

If you already know some of the language, take the placement test. It asks
progressively harder questions, finds your level, and unlocks the modules you
have already covered. If you are starting from zero, skip it — the first
module assumes no prior knowledge.

## Build the habit

Lessons are about five minutes. The review queue is what makes the material
stick, so a short session every day beats a long one every week.
